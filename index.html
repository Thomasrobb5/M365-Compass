M365 Compass is a **client-side purely** application. Your data never leaves your browser ecosystem except to communicate directly with the **Microsoft Graph API**.

  • No external database or backend storage is used.
  • Data is cached locally in your browser (IndexedDB) for performance.
  • Authentication is handled securely via MSAL (Microsoft Authentication Library).
